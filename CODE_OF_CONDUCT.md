# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private data, and disruptive behavior are not accepted in project spaces.

Discuss technical decisions on their merits, assume good intent while asking for evidence, and give contributors a reasonable opportunity to correct mistakes. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. The repository owner must configure a private contact before the public launch. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest avoided.
